I am a theoretical biophysicist working on self-assembly and active matter.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [group of Martin Lenz](https://www.lptms.universite-paris-saclay.fr/membres/mlenz/index.html) of
[LPTMS](https://www.lptms.universite-paris-saclay.fr/) in Orsay, France.
Currently, I mainly study the equilibrium self-assembly of three-dimensional particles
with complex anisotropic interactions.

During my PhD, I worked under the supervision of [Ramin
Golestanian](https://www.ds.mpg.de/lmp) at the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and
Self-Organization in Göttingen, Germany. As a member of the group of [Jaime
Agudo-Canalejo](https://www.agudo-canalejo.com/home) (now at UCL), I used analytical and
computational tools to study mixtures of enzyme-like catalytically active
particles.
