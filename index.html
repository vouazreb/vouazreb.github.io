Welcome!

I am a theoretical biophysicist working on self-assembly and active matter.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the group of Martin Lenz of LPTMS in Orsay, France. Currently, I mainly study the equilibrium self-assembly of three-dimensional particles with complex anisotropic interactions.

During my PhD, I worked under the supervision of Ramin Golestanian at the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization in Göttingen, Germany. As a member of the group of Jaime Agudo-Canalejo (now at UCL), I used analytical and computational tools to study mixtures of enzyme-like catalytically active particles.
